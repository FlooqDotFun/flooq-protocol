# Flooq Whitepaper

## Vision

Markets move in loops: attention builds, passes hands, and comes back around. Flooq takes that shape literally. Its mark is one stroke that turns back on itself four times, with no seam and no join. The vision is a token whose public face is as continuous and as honest as its logo: every claim stands on a record, and every record is either stated or marked pending.

## Mission

1. Give holders a single place to read what has actually been stated, and nothing that has not.
2. Show the surrounding Solana market live, so Flooq is always seen in context rather than in isolation.
3. Keep the contract address, social links and any other outside fact switched off until the team files them.
4. Build in the open, one record at a time.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Signal.** Brand, site and register go live. Channels open as soon as their handles are stated.

**Phase 2: Current.** The contract is announced and tracked on the live tape beside the rest of the Solana market.

**Phase 3: Tide.** Community drives and tooling built around the ribbon, in the open.

**Phase 4: Delta.** The line branches into partners, listings and integrations, each entered in the register before it is mentioned anywhere else.
